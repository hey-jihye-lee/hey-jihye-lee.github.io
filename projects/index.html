Older Adults × Social Robots

Social robots in everyday life

I study how older adults interpret, use, and form relationships with social robots in real-world settings, and how caregivers, family, and introduction practices shape sustained engagement.

Home deployment · HRI · Healthy aging · Social connection

Everyday Social Connection

Understanding differences in relationships and support needs

Current studies use network-based clustering, ecological momentary assessment, and longitudinal panel data to understand heterogeneity in social connection and changes within everyday life.

Network typology · 14-day EMA · Longitudinal analysis

Digital Health × Equity

Designing interventions beyond average outcomes

I use computational approaches to examine how preventive interventions can allocate limited resources while accounting for differences in media use, access, and vulnerability.

Agent-based modeling · Personalization · Equity

Social AI × Identity

Identity, alignment, trust, and bias in human–AI interaction

This work examines how artificial agents present identity and how AI systems respond differently to the identities of the people they interact with.

Artificial identity · LLM sycophancy · AI counseling
