Publications, presentations, appointments, and other research updates.

Nov 2026

Conference. Presenting work on social relationship trajectories and socially assistive robot support at the Gerontological Society of America Annual Scientific Meeting. This is the first public outing for the network-based clustering work behind the relationship-supportive robots project.

Nov 2026

Talk. Our agent-based modeling study on channel allocation and equity in preventive campaigns for older adults was accepted for an oral presentation at the AMIA 2026 Annual Symposium in Dallas, Texas.

Oct 2026

Publication. The same study was published in the Journal of Medical Internet Research (doi:10.2196/88429). The model compares channel allocation, personalization, and loss framing under explicit budget and equity guardrails, and finds that equity-focused personalization improves both uptake and distributional equity.

Oct 2026

Under review. Our work on ingroup sycophancy in large language models, led by Dongjun Kim at GIST, has been submitted to AAAI-27. It asks whether models conform differently to in-group and out-group users under minimal group conditions. See the project card.

Conference. Presented a typology of older adults' social networks and its implications for social robot intervention strategies at the 5th World Conference on Humanities and Social Sciences.

Designing Artificial Identity: The Identity Design Framework and Research Agenda, a collaboration with fourteen co-authors led by Karla Bransky, was published at ACM DIS 2026 in Singapore (DOI).
