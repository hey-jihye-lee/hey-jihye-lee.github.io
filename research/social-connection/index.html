Research Interest 02

Everyday Social Connection

Social connection is not a fixed individual trait. I am interested in how relationships, interaction, and support needs differ across people and fluctuate within the same person's everyday life.

← all research interests

Why everyday variation matters

Much of my current work asks what becomes visible when we stop treating older adults as a single user group. Network structure, relationship quality, daily interaction, and moments of disconnection can point to very different support needs.

This interest connects descriptive work—understanding patterns of social relationships—to intervention questions about whether, when, and for whom technology should step in.

Questions I'm exploring

  • What kinds of social relationship patterns exist among older adults?
  • How does social experience change across moments and days?
  • Which differences matter when designing social support technologies?
