Research Interest 04

Social AI × Identity

As AI becomes a social actor, identity operates in both directions: systems present identities of their own, while also responding to the identities they perceive in users. I study the opportunities and risks created by that interaction.

← all research interests

Identity on both sides of interaction

One part of this work examines how artificial agents are designed to present identity and persona. Another asks how AI systems change their responses depending on who they believe the user is.

Together, these projects raise a critical boundary question for adaptive social technology: when is responding differently to people appropriate tailoring, and when does it reproduce social bias or relational favoritism?

Questions I'm exploring

  • How should artificial identity and persona be designed and reported?
  • Do language models align differently with in-group and out-group users?
  • When does social adaptation become bias?
