Research Interest 03

Digital Health × Equity

I am interested in health technologies and interventions that work not only on average, but across people whose access, media use, resources, and vulnerabilities differ.

← all research interests

Beyond average outcomes

My digital health work asks how limited intervention resources should be allocated when populations are heterogeneous. This includes computational work on preventive campaigns as well as human-subject research on AI-mediated counseling.

Across these settings, equity is not an afterthought: it is part of the design problem. A strategy that maximizes overall reach can still leave particular groups behind.

Questions I'm exploring

  • How should interventions adapt to differences in access, channels, and vulnerability?
  • When does personalization improve support, and who benefits?
  • How can effectiveness and distributional equity be considered together?
