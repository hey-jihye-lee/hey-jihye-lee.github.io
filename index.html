Research program

The questions that drive my research

Rather than organizing my work by technology, I follow a set of questions about difference, interaction, and support.

01

How do people's needs differ—and change?

Relationships, everyday contexts, and needs differ across people and can change within the same person over time.

Social networks · EMA · Longitudinal research
02

What happens when people meet technology?

I examine how people experience, interpret, trust, and communicate with emerging technologies in real contexts.

Social robots · AI counseling · Identity
03

How should support differ?

I ask how differences in people's relationships, contexts, and needs can inform more appropriate forms of support.

Intervention design · Digital health · Participatory research
↔

A critical mirror

When does tailoring become bias?

If technology responds differently to different people, when is that responsiveness—and when does it become bias?

LLM behavior · Artificial identity · Responsible AI
