Jihye Lee

Human–Technology Interaction Researcher
Social robotics, digital health, social AI, and healthy aging

I study how people experience emerging technologies in everyday life, with a particular focus on older adults, social connection, and well-being. My work spans social robots, digital health, and social AI.

I am especially interested in how people's relationships, contexts, and needs differ, and what those differences mean for technologies designed to support them.

Background

Before joining Seoul National University, I was a researcher and lead project coordinator at the AIX School of Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology (GIST), where I coordinated an AI-based analysis platform that turned international news into evidence for national policymaking.

I received my Ph.D. in Communication from Chonnam National University in 2024. My dissertation examined interaction and communication between social robots and older adults, asking whether social robots can reduce loneliness in later life. Before that, I completed an M.S. in Arts and Cultural Management at Chung-Ang University with a thesis on the strategy and competitiveness of Korean webtoon platforms.

Prior to academia, I spent six years at the Korea Communications Agency managing national ICT R&D funding programs and conducting policy research on AI and digital media for government stakeholders. That experience still shapes how I think about the path from research findings to policy and practice.

Teaching and mentoring

At Hanyang University ERICA I teach Digital Health Design, which follows user-centered design for health technologies from needs assessment through prototyping and evaluation. At SNU and GIST I mentor thirteen master's and doctoral students in study design, field research, and quantitative and computational analysis.

I review for the ACM/IEEE International Conference on Human–Robot Interaction (HRI 2027) and co-organized a workshop on mechanical motion and affect at HCI Korea 2026. See my CV for funding, awards, and service, and my publications for recent work.
